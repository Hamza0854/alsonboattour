Private Sunset Tour

Albanian sunsets are a hidden gem, and there's no better way to experience them than from the sea. As the golden sun dips behind the cliffs of the Karaburun Peninsula, the sky transforms into a breathtaking masterpiece, creating an unforgettable moment.

Join our Private Sunset Tour and cruise along Vlora Bay and Karaburun on a luxurious Alson speedboat, guided by Captain Lenci, an experienced local seafarer.
